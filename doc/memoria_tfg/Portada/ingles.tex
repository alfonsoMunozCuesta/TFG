\section*{Extension and Improvement of an Interactive Dashboard for Survival Analysis Applied to University Dropout}

\noindent
Muñoz Cuesta, Alfonso(2026). Trabajo de Fin de Grado. Córdoba: Universidad de Córdoba.
\section*{Abstract}
This Final Degree Project aims to extend and improve an interactive web application focused on survival analysis applied to university dropout. Academic dropout affects students' trajectories and the planning of degree programmes. In this context, survival analysis makes it possible to study when dropout occurs and which factors are associated with a higher risk.

The developed solution builds on a previous system and extends it with new features for loading, validating and preprocessing academic data. The application supports Kaplan-Meier, Cox regression, the Log-Rank test, and Exponential and Weibull models, while also incorporating Random Survival Forest to analyse risk profiles. These techniques are presented in an interactive dashboard that supports visual exploration and comparison between methods.

In addition, the project introduces usability and maintainability improvements, such as multilingual support, modular organisation, PDF report export and automatic interpretation through local artificial intelligence. As a result, the tool helps users understand the results obtained and provides a practical environment to support the study of university dropout.

\section*{Keywords}
Survival analysis, university dropout, interactive dashboard, statistical models, Random Survival Forest, local artificial intelligence.
